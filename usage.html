It's not that the app "picks" computers — it's environment differences. Work through these in order and you'll usually find it in 3 minutes.

熊猫桌宠伙伴是养在手机桌面上的宠物熊猫：

"浮在其他应用上"是安卓的敏感能力，默认关闭。没开权限时，熊猫不会出现在桌面和别的应用上方——这几乎就是"装上后看不到宠物"的唯一原因。

设置 → 应用 → 熊猫桌宠伙伴 → 权限 → 悬浮窗 → 允许。

各品牌菜单名略不同：小米/红米「悬浮窗」，华为/荣耀「悬浮窗 / 显示在其他应用上层」，OPPO/一加/realme「显示悬浮窗」，vivo/iQOO「悬浮窗」。找不到就用手机设置里的搜索框搜"悬浮窗"。

3）开好之后

回到熊猫 App 首页，按提示点一下，熊猫就飘到屏幕上了。

A panda that wanders, parachutes and chats, living on your phone screen. This guide gets you started in 3 minutes.

1. What is it

Panda Companion is a pet panda that lives on your phone's screen:

  • Only the panda itself is visible on a fully transparent background — it floats above WeChat, videos or your home screen without blocking anything;
  • It wanders along the bottom of the screen, hops up, floats down like a parachute, and does little things (blinks, stretches…) when tired;

  • It collects or uploads no personal data. The only network use is fetching public quotes — built-in content works offline too, so it's safe to use.

2. Download & install

  1. Download DeskBud_Panda_Android_vVERSION.apk from the release channel (a single installer, about 50 MB).
  2. Open the APK — the system will ask "allow apps from unknown sources?" — choose Allow (wording varies by brand, but it's always this step).
  3. Tap Open once installed.

Apps that are not on an app store get one extra prompt from Android by default — that's normal security behavior, not a problem.

3. The key step: grant the "overlay" permission (without it you can't see the panda)

"Display over other apps" is a sensitive Android capability and is off by default. Without it, the panda will not appear above your home screen or other apps — that is almost always the only reason you "can't see the pet after installing".

1) On the very first launch

If a "overlay permission needed" prompt appears, just tap Go to settings / Allow.

2) No prompt, or it stops working later — turn it on manually

Settings → Apps → Panda Companion → Permissions → Display over other apps → Allow.

Menu names differ by brand: Xiaomi/Redmi "Display over other apps"; Huawei/Honor "Display over other apps / Appear on top"; OPPO/OnePlus/realme "Display pop-up windows"; vivo/iQOO "Display over other apps". Can't find it? Search "overlay" (悬浮窗) in your phone's settings search box.

3) After enabling

Return to the Panda app home screen, follow the on-screen hint and tap once — the panda will float onto your screen.

4. First run: you may be blocked once (common, don't panic)

Small apps that skip app stores may trigger a "unknown source / risky app" warning from your phone's security center or app store — it's Android's generic notice for newly installed packages. Choose "Install anyway / Continue".

Some vendor security apps may block and delete it outright: go to your security center, trust / whitelist the installer, then download and install once more — it works fine afterwards.

5. How to play

  • Press and drag the panda: move it anywhere;
  • Single-tap the panda: pause / resume activity (tap while it's walking and it will stop and wait for you);
  • Single-tap after landing or after dragging: it jumps up and floats down slowly like a parachute (press and hold to charge — the longer you hold, the higher it jumps);
  • Long-press the panda: open the menu — walk left / walk right, circle the screen, About & version, Privacy, 中/EN language switch, quit;
  • It also wanders on its own: pacing along the bottom of the screen, sometimes taking longer routes or curves, and resting in place with little animations when tired.

Idle animations are random — wait a while and you'll see it switch tricks.

6. The bubble above its head

  • It occasionally bubbles a panda quote. Fresh lines are fetched online automatically, but built-in ones work offline too — it plays fine without a network;
  • Text auto-switches black/white to stay readable on light or dark screens — that's by design.

7. FAQ

Q: The panda disappeared?
A: Nine times out of ten the overlay permission was quietly revoked (some systems turn it off after a reboot). Re-enable it as in section 3, then open the app and tap once — it comes right back.

Q: After locking the screen, the panda is gone when I wake it?
A: To save power, the system hides overlays while the screen is locked; it restores itself when you unlock and return to the home screen. Normal, not broken.

Q: It stops responding / looks killed after a while?
A: The system's power-saving cleaned it up in the background. In your phone's "Battery / Background management", set the panda to allow background running / ignore battery optimization (Xiaomi: Settings → App settings → Permissions → Autostart, turn it on), then reopen the app to restore it.

Q: The panda blocks videos or typing?
A: Drag it to a corner, or tap once to pause it; tap again when you need it back.

Q: How do I know if it's the latest version?
A: Long-press the panda → the version number is in About; get newer versions from the original download channel.
