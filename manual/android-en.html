2Set Up in 3 Steps

Takes less than a minute — your pet lands on screen right after.

1

Install

Open the installer and tap Install. If your phone warns about "unknown apps", just allow it once when prompted.

2

Open the app

On first launch you'll be asked to allow "Display over other apps". That's the key that lets your pet float on screen — it must be turned on.

3

Tap Start

Pick your pet and tap Start — it drops from the sky onto your screen.

4Common Questions

Tap a question for the answer — most issues take 10 seconds to fix.

I opened the app but can't see my pet
Nine times out of ten it's the "Display over other apps" permission not granted. Go to Settings → Apps → DeskBud and turn it on (on Xiaomi phones it's under "Other permissions", called "Display pop-up windows"). Then go back to the app and tap Start again.
My pet disappeared after a while
Your phone's battery cleaner probably closed it. Just tap DeskBud in the notification panel to bring it back. To keep it around, lock DeskBud in Recents / add it to the battery whitelist.
Does it block my touches?
No. If it's in the way, just hold and drag it elsewhere. It never blocks your buttons or notifications.
Does it drain the battery?
It's very light — you'll barely notice it over a full day. If you'd rather save every drop, exit from the notification panel when you don't need it, and bring it back anytime.
Will my pet survive a reinstall?
Uninstalling clears everything. Just reinstall and pick your pet again — it only takes a minute.
The install prompt never shows up
Some phones silently cancel install confirmations while the screen is off. Unlock the screen first, then install. On a few models you may also need to allow "Install via USB" in settings.
💡 Tip: Your pet can't be broken, no matter how you play with it. If you spot a quirky little move, that's not a bug — that's personality.
