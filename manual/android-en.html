DeskBud Pets

Little friends that live on your phone screen

Arrives on its own · Walks, jumps, climbs · Talks in bubbles

1It's already there

Install, open the app, and your pet shows up by itself — no button to press.

1

Install

Open the package and install. If your phone warns about unknown apps, allow it once.

2

Grant one permission

On first launch you'll be asked to allow Display over other apps (called "Show floating windows" on Xiaomi). This is the key that lets your pet stay on the home screen — please allow it.

3

It appears by itself

Go back to your home screen — your little friend is already waiting.

2The console has four things

Top to bottom, that's all there is on the app screen.

🧩

Pet wall (tap to toggle)

Up to 10 pets per screen (5 per row, 2 rows). Tap a pet to send it out or call it home; swipe sideways when you have more than 10.

▶️

Start / Stop

Usually you can ignore it. Tap Stop to let them rest; tap Start to bring them back.

🎚️

Do-not-disturb lever

Each tap cycles: Off → Focus mode (smaller, stays to the side, quiet) → Typing guard (moves out of the way when the keyboard shows). Default is Off.

↻ ⓘ ⏻ EN

Four small buttons, top right

Update, About, Quit — plus one-tap Chinese/English switch.

5Updating

Tap "Update" in the top-right corner — it does the rest.

When a new version exists, it downloads inside the app and asks you to install — no browser, no hunting for the file.

If it says "You're on the latest version", you already are.

6FAQ

Tap a question; most issues are solved in 10 seconds.

I opened the app but can't see my pet
Almost always the "Display over other apps" permission is missing. Go to Settings → Apps → DeskBud and turn it on (Xiaomi lists it under "Other permissions" as "Show floating windows"), then reopen the app.
Will it block my taps?
No. If it's in the way, press and drag it aside; if it's too busy, switch the lever to Focus mode or Typing guard.
Update does nothing / install fails
The first in-app install asks whether DeskBud may install apps — choose Allow. If the screen is off, the confirmation is cancelled, so unlock first, then tap Install. Still stuck? Grab the latest from the download page.
My pet disappeared after a while
Usually the system cleaned up background apps. Just open DeskBud again and it comes back; to keep it alive, lock it in your recent-apps list.
Where do the pets go after I tap Quit?
Quit sends them home and closes everything, like closing a desktop program. Open the app again to bring them back.
Does it drain the battery?
Very little — a full day is usually unnoticeable. If you care, tap Stop or Quit when you don't need it.
💡 Tip: Your pet can't be dropped or broken — play rough. If it does something odd, that's not a bug, that's personality.
